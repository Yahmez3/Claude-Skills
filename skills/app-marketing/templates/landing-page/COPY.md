# Landing page copy — checklist

Do not ship until every answer exists.

## Hero

- [ ] **Headline** (≤10 words): Who it's for + what they get.
  > Weak: "The best way to manage money"
  > Strong: "Envelope budgeting for people who hate budgeting"
- [ ] **Subhead** (≤20 words): Expand the headline. Address one skepticism.
- [ ] **Primary CTA**: "Get it on App Store" / "Join waitlist" / "Try free" — ONE option.
- [ ] **Microcopy under CTA**: remove friction. "No credit card" / "No spam" / "iOS only today, Android soon"

## Proof

- [ ] **Social proof row**: 3-5 outlet logos OR user quote with name+photo OR "1,200 on the waitlist" — real numbers, nothing fake.

## Body sections

- [ ] **Feature 1**: Benefit-led headline, one sentence, one screenshot/GIF.
- [ ] **Feature 2**: same pattern.
- [ ] **Feature 3**: same pattern.

## Trust

- [ ] Privacy summary (one sentence) or link to full policy
- [ ] Pricing stated clearly — never hide the price

## Final CTA

- [ ] Repeat the primary CTA
- [ ] Different supporting line than the hero (e.g., hero = "Try it", final = "Still reading? Start free.")

## Footer

- [ ] Privacy, Terms, Press, Contact
- [ ] Company name + year
- [ ] Social links if they add proof (not if they're empty)

## What NOT to include

- Autoplay video with sound
- Carousel of testimonials (replace with 1-2 strong ones)
- "Revolutionary", "unleash", "unlock", "reimagine" — category tells
- A chat widget before you have volume to justify it
